Our story

We started SWITCH out of a simple frustration: the heaviest tees never fit loose, and the loose ones never felt heavy. So we made the ones we couldn’t find.

Chapter 01

The idea

SWITCH began with a group of friends and a wardrobe full of clothes that almost worked. Tees that shrank. Shorts that sat wrong. Hoodies too thin for a cold morning.

We wanted pieces with weight, cut with room, built to be lived in. So we started sketching. The name is the whole idea: flip it on and get dressed like you mean it, whatever the day looks like.

Chapter 02

The fabric

Everything starts with the cloth. It feels stiff on day one and softer with every wash, never thinner. We choose weight over everything else.
