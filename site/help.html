FAQ

Common questions.

How do I order?

Pick a piece, choose a colour and size, and add it to your bag. When you are ready, press Order on WhatsApp. Your order opens as a ready-written message in our chat. We confirm availability, agree delivery and share payment details with you there.

Do I pay on the website?

No. There is no online checkout. You pay after we confirm your order on WhatsApp, so you always know exactly what you are getting and when it arrives.

What are the pieces made of?

Tees are 280gsm combed cotton jersey, jorts are 14oz rigid cotton denim and hoodies are brushed-back fleece. Full details are in the product view of every piece.

What does “upcoming” mean?

Upcoming pieces are not on sale yet. You can preview them in 3D and tap Notify me to get early access on WhatsApp the moment they drop.

Are the colours accurate?

The 3D view is built to match the real garment as closely as a screen allows. Screens vary, so if a colour matters to you, ask us for a photo on WhatsApp.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes, until it is dispatched. Message us on WhatsApp with your order details as soon as you can.

Delivery

Getting it to you.

We arrange delivery with you on WhatsApp once your order is confirmed. The delivery fee and timing depend on your location, and we will give you both before you pay.

  • Tell us your name and area in the bag so we can quote you straight away.
  • Most orders are dispatched within a few working days of payment.
  • We will message you when your order is on its way.

Returns

Not right?

If something is faulty or the wrong item, message us on WhatsApp within 7 days of receiving it and we will make it right with a swap or refund.

  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and have their tags attached.
  • Please include your order details and a photo of the issue.
  • Sale and final-drop pieces are not eligible for change-of-mind returns.
